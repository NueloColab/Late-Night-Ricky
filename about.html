About

Late Night Ricky

From late-night sets in London's underground clubs to headlining stages across Europe and beyond.

What started as a passion for music in small basement venues turned into an impressive journey with residencies in cities like London, Ibiza, Miami, and Dubai. Playing events at fashion weeks, Grand Prix celebrations, exclusive parties, open-air festivals, and the most sought-after clubs in the world.

Not only is Late Night Ricky one of the most in-demand open-format DJs in the game, his own productions and remixes have gained support from major artists and renowned radio stations.

With many unreleased tracks in the works, plans to further expand his brand and collaborations with other artists, there is a lot more to come this year.
