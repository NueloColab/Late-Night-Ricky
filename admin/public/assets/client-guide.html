How It Works in Detail

  • Create a Quote — Add line items (services, fees, travel, etc.). Toggle VAT and discounts. Set payment terms (e.g., 50% deposit, 50% on completion). Every quote gets a unique number like QT-001.
  • Send to Client — Click "Send Quote". They receive a branded Late Night Ricky email with your logo. The email contains a secure link to view and accept the quote.
  • Client Accepts — They click the link, see their quote beautifully presented, and click Accept. They get a confirmation. You get notified.
  • Auto-Convert to Invoice — The moment they accept, the platform creates a matching invoice with the same line items, totals, and payment schedule. No retyping anything.
  • Invoice Email Sent — The client automatically receives their invoice by email with a payment link and your bank details.
  • Client Pays — They follow the payment page, upload proof of payment (screenshot/photo), and submit.
  • You Confirm — In your admin, you see the payment confirmation with their receipt. Mark it confirmed. Done.
