How It Works

  • Create a Quote — Add line items, toggle VAT and discounts, set payment terms. Every quote gets a unique number like QT-001.
  • Send to Client — Click "Send Quote". They receive a branded Late Night Ricky email with your logo and a secure accept link.
  • Client Accepts — They view their quote beautifully presented and click Accept. Confirmation sent to both sides.
  • Auto-Convert to Invoice — The platform instantly creates a matching invoice. No retyping anything.
  • Invoice Email Sent — Client receives their invoice with payment link and your bank details.
  • Client Pays — They upload proof of payment (screenshot/photo) through their invoice page.
  • You Confirm — In your admin, see the payment confirmation with their receipt. Mark it confirmed. Done.
